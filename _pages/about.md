<span class='anchor' id='about-me'></span>

I am **Guiyu Ma**, an AI Researcher working on large language models (LLMs) and multimodal agents, with the goal of turning frontier model advances into AI products that improve everyday life. I received my M.S. in Control Science and Engineering from Xi'an Jiaotong University in 2026 and my B.E. in Digital Media Technology from Central China Normal University in 2023.

My research focuses on building vision-language-action and GUI agents that perceive visual interfaces, reason over long horizons, and complete tasks across mobile applications and complex interactive environments. Broadly, my interests span MLLM/LLM post-training (SFT & RL), agent frameworks, and systematic evaluation.

Feel free to reach out at <a href='mailto:hi.guiyu.ma@gmail.com' target='_self'>hi.guiyu.ma@gmail.com</a>.
